clearance: intake that reads the fax

A configurable qualification engine for DME orders. Built for a fictional customer, Cascade Sleep & Respiratory. All data synthetic.

Every PAP order is a fax packet a coordinator has to read, check, and chase

  • ~1,500 new PAP orders a month, six intake coordinators (assumed)
  • ~18 minutes of hands-on time per order: find pages, key fields, check Medicare criteria, look up eligibility (assumed)
  • Defects surface late: an unsigned order, a face-to-face note dated after the sleep test, an AHI of 9 with no documented symptom. Each is a denial or a recoupment waiting to happen.

  • Needs-info orders get a drafted fax back to the office, each item citing the LCD section.
